Privacy Policy

How Efxion Technology Solutions collects, uses and protects information provided through our website and services.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Introduction

Efxion Technology Solutions respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, contacting us, applying for opportunities or interacting with our services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it and the measures we take to protect it.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Email address and other information submitted through contact forms.
  • Information provided when applying for a career opportunity.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when requesting a service or discussing a project.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device information and website usage information.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our website may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests.
  • Communicate with prospective and existing clients.
  • Process and review job applications.
  • Provide and improve our technology services.
  • Maintain and improve the security and functionality of our website.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Job Applications

If you apply for a position through our careers portal, information you submit may include your name, contact information, professional qualifications, work experience and uploaded application documents.

This information is used for recruitment and evaluation purposes and may be reviewed by authorized members of Efxion Technology Solutions.

5. Contact Forms

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, the information you provide may be stored so that we can respond to your request and maintain appropriate business records.

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card information or other highly sensitive information through general website forms.

6. Cookies and Technical Information

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies to support authentication, security, functionality and user preferences.

We may also receive basic technical information associated with visits to our website, such as browser and device information.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party technology providers to support certain website functions, such as email delivery, hosting, analytics, security or other business operations.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services, subject to their own policies and applicable requirements.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business purposes, dispute resolution, security and applicable legal requirements.

10. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of information you have provided to us.

Requests concerning personal information can be directed to Efxion Technology Solutions through our contact page.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and professional services are not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through our general website forms.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements or business practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle information, please contact Efxion Technology Solutions through our contact page .
